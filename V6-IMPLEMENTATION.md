## Public truth constraints

The public site does not currently claim:

- named customers or client case studies,
- founder/person identity,
- private technology stack or hardware as a public production claim,
- unpublished benchmark results,
- street address,
- unverified external social profiles.

## Benchmark rule

A numerical result should only be published with enough context to understand and reproduce the test: environment, versions, parameters, input data, metric, repetitions and limitations.

## Content rule

Do not mass-generate thin pages. New content must have a distinct intent and connect to the entity, solution, technology, research or answer graph.
